# AboutTextTests

Unit tests for `RiftRoulette/Lobby/AboutText`.

- The lines carry the current betting numbers (start 100, kill 100, assist
  50), the close time passed in, and the `/reserve`, `/heroban` and `/mark`
  prices, in seven lines.
- No line says chips.
- `MirrorLines` say everyone has the same hero and build, with no betting
  or `/reserve` line.
