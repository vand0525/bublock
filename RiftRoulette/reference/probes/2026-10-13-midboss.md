## Analysis

- **Scheduler route.** Fails as configured. With `citadel_npc_spawn_enabled 0`
  and the game clock already past 1 s, the override did nothing and
  `NextMidBossSpawnTime` stayed 0. Not isolated: either the NPC switch
  blocks it or the initial-spawn override is only read before the first
  spawn. Not needed now, since the direct route works.
- **Direct spawn works and is the route to build on.** No crash. The
  created boss is a full mid-boss: designer `npc_super_neutral`, class
  `CNPC_MidBoss`, team 4, both vdata modifiers, the game's own
  `midboss_respawned` event, kill credit, the crystal drop and pickup.
  The pit rule applies to it (hits from outside arrive with
  `NotInMidbossPit` and 0 damage).
- **Starting health grows with game time, not time alive.** 16,120 at
  959 s and 17,485 at 1,406 s are 13,000 + 195 × game minutes (16 and 23,
  rounded). In a 2-hour lobby a fresh boss starts near 36,400. Writing
  health after spawn removes that.
- **Health and max health.** `Health` writes hold. `m_iMaxHealth` writes
  hold and the effective max follows, but they don't clamp current health,
  and a new boss gets the vdata max again. Set the max, then the health,
  on every spawn. Regen is about 15 health/s (8,221 to 9,411 in 79 s).
- **Damage resistance is a threshold, applied before `OnTakeDamage`.**
  With `midboss_modifier_damage_resistance` on, every pit hit reached the
  hook already at 0, so scaling 0 does nothing. With the modifier removed,
  hits arrived with real damage and the ×5 scale applied (390 → 1,950).
  Removing the modifier is the fix for long lobbies (its threshold grows
  5 per game minute); the scale then tunes damage on top.
- **Scream.** Not triggered by writing health below 50%, nor by shooting
  a boss already below half. It likely needs damage to cross 50%; to test
  on a full-health boss with the resistance removed.
- **`/midboss_kill` is broken.** `TakeDamage` with `ForceDeath` and
  health + 1 did nothing (no `entity_killed`, health unchanged). Likely
  causes: the pit rule (the attacker, the admin's hero, was not in the
  pit) or the resistance modifier, which was on. Fix candidates: remove
  the resistance first, add `IgnoreResistances`, and use the boss itself
  as attacker when the caller is outside the pit (that loses team credit,
  so the crystal team needs checking). Follow-up: the command was
  dropped instead (a mode never needs to kill the boss; players do) and
  replaced by `/midboss_ready`.
- **Crystal.** Spawns the moment the boss dies, 500 units above the pit
  (z -252), team 4, falls to the floor. The killer's team (Amber) got the
  count. `AmberRejuvCount` rose three times over 6 s, then the crystal
  entity was removed with `m_bPickedUp` set, and the count went back to 0
  about 180 s later (the 180 s rejuv buff). What the three steps are
  (three touches, three buff stages) is not known.
- **Log label bug.** `Mid-boss counts changed` prints `AmberRejuv=Amber=0`:
  the template names (`{Amber}`) differ from the written labels. Fixed
  (placeholders renamed).
